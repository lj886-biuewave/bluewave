# bluewave
RISC-V,AI,CMOS

This repository records my self-study and hands-on practice in **RISC-V architecture, GPU & AI hardware acceleration, digital IC design, and CMOS fundamentals**.

I am a junior student majoring in Computer Science at Liaoning University, Shenyang, China.
My research interest focuses on hardware architecture and algorithm-hardware co-design for edge AI chips.
I explore RTL design with Verilog/SystemVerilog, EDA verification, FPGA prototyping, and CMOS circuit theory.
The long-term goal is to build a RISC-V heterogeneous SoC integrated with a lightweight GPU for real-time motion/gesture recognition and peripheral control.

All source codes, notes, simulation testbenches and project documents will be maintained here.
